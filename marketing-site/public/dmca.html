What a notice must include

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. The copyrighted work you say is infringed.
  3. The material you say infringes it, with enough detail for us to find it.
  4. Your name, address, phone number and email address.
  5. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by the owner, its agent or the law.
  6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and that you are the owner or are authorized to act for the owner.

What we do

When we get a valid notice, we remove or disable the material quickly and tell the user who uploaded it.

Counter-notice

If your material was removed by mistake, you can send a counter-notice to the agent. Include your signature, the removed material and where it was, a statement under penalty of perjury that it was removed by mistake or misidentification, your name, address and phone number, and your consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your address (or for Dallas County, Texas, if you are outside the United States). We may restore the material in 10 to 14 business days unless the person who complained files a lawsuit.

Repeat infringers

We end the accounts of users who infringe copyright repeatedly.

False notices

Anyone who knowingly makes a false claim in a notice or counter-notice can be liable for damages under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).
