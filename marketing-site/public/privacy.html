Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-14 · Effective November 14, 2026

This policy explains what Samad Alam, doing business as Field Pro ("we"), collects through this website and the hosted Field Pro workspace, and what we do with it.

This website

This website sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, no advertising tags, no tracking pixels and no session recording. It loads no fonts or scripts from Google or other outside sources. The estimate tool on the home page runs only in your browser and sends nothing to us.

Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, to run and protect the site.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your card details, email address and billing details. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe shares with us the purchase status, amount, email address and the date and time you accepted our terms. We use this to deliver your download, handle refunds and keep tax and business records. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to the data it holds.

The hosted workspace

When you use the hosted workspace, we collect:

  • Your name, email address and account ID from Microsoft sign-in.
  • Company details you enter, such as legal name, regions, tax settings and logo.
  • Job data you enter or upload, such as customer names and addresses, measurements, photos, estimates, proposals and invoices.
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is required and is not used for ads.
  • Security and error logs, such as IP address and request times.

The roof map loads satellite imagery from Esri's servers, and address searches are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service. Those providers receive your IP address and the map area or address you look up. Email notifications are sent through our email provider. The workspace and its database are hosted on Render.

How we use information

We use information only to provide Field Pro, deliver purchases, send messages you need about your account and jobs, prevent fraud and abuse, fix problems, and meet legal duties. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for targeted advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers named above, and only so they can do their work for us. We also share it when the law requires, or to protect our rights and the safety of others. If the business is sold, the information may move to the new owner under this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep account and job data while your company's account is active. We delete it within 90 days after the account closes, except records we must keep for tax, legal or dispute reasons. Purchase records are kept for 7 years for tax purposes.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your personal information. Email us from the address on your account. We answer within 45 days. We will not treat you differently for making a request. Residents of states with privacy laws, such as Texas and California, have these rights under those laws.

Children

Field Pro is for adults running a business. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

Security

We use encryption in transit, encrypted file storage, access controls and audit logs. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

Location

Field Pro is run from Texas, United States, and data is stored in the United States.

Changes and contact

We will post changes here and update the version date. For questions or requests, email samad.alam481470sa@gmail.com.
