Estimate Disclaimer

Version 2026-11-14 · Effective November 14, 2026

Field Pro calculates measurements, material quantities and prices from the numbers you enter, from photos, and from third-party map data. The results are estimates only.

  • Results can be wrong if inputs, photos, calibration or map data are wrong or old.
  • Results are not a survey, an engineering design, an inspection or a binding quote.
  • Unit prices are the prices you enter. Field Pro does not guarantee market prices.
  • Map imagery may be out of date and may not match the property today.

Your responsibilities

  • Measure and verify every job on site before you bid, order material or start work.
  • Follow local building codes and get every required permit.
  • Call 811 before any digging or concrete cutting to locate underground utilities.
  • Use licensed engineers, surveyors or inspectors wherever the law requires them.
  • Review every proposal and invoice before you send it to a customer.

The home page estimate tool is only a simple example: square feet or linear feet times the price you type in. It is not a quote from Field Pro. Our liability is limited as stated in the Terms of Service.
