# Field Pro data breach plan

This is the written plan the Privacy policy points to. It says who does what when personal
information may have been exposed, and how quickly. It is the company's stated policy, not a
description of a regulator's deadlines: where the law sets a deadline, the plan says so in
plain words instead of quoting a number.

## What counts as a breach

Any event where personal information held by Field Pro may have been seen, taken, changed or
lost by someone who should not have it — for example an account break-in, a leaked database
backup, a lost device with workspace data, or an email sent to the wrong address. A suspected
breach is treated as a breach until the assessment says otherwise.

## Who does what

- **Detection.** Automated alerts (failed sign-ins, unusual access, error alerts via
  `ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL`), the audit log, and reports from users or workspace owners. Anyone who
  suspects a breach tells the workspace owner and Field Pro at
  samad.alam481470sa@gmail.com, with what happened and when.
- **Assessment.** The Field Pro team (the workspace owner, Samad Alam, is the point of contact)
  works out what information was involved, whose it was, and how it got out. The team can read
  the audit log, session records and request logs; those logs are designed not to contain
  tenant content.
- **Containment.** Revoke the affected sessions, change the affected credentials, close the
  hole that let it happen, and keep a record of every step.
- **Notification.** The Field Pro team notifies the affected users and, where the law requires
  it, the relevant regulator. Users are told what happened, what information was involved,
  what we did about it, and what they should do.

## Timelines we commit to

- **Assessment within 72 hours of discovery.** Within 72 hours of learning about a suspected
  breach, we complete the assessment: what was involved, whose it was, and how it happened.
- **User notification without unreasonable delay.** Once the assessment shows personal
  information was affected, we notify the affected users without unreasonable delay — by email
  and by a notice in the application — and we do not wait for the full investigation to finish
  if people need to act now (for example, to change a password).
- **Regulator notification where the law requires it.** If the law requires a regulator to be
  told, we tell them within the period that law sets. The plan does not quote a single number
  here because the period depends on the law that applies to the people affected.

## After the breach

- Write down what happened, what was affected, what was done and when, and keep that record.
- Fix the cause and check whether the same weakness exists anywhere else.
- Tell affected users what changed, so they can judge for themselves whether the fix is enough.

## Limits, stated plainly

- Email delivery is not connected yet, so notification today is by a notice in the
  application and by direct contact from the Field Pro team; email notification starts when
  the mailer is connected.
- The plan covers information Field Pro holds. Information a company holds about its own
  customers is that company's responsibility first: Field Pro tells the company, and the
  company tells its customers.
